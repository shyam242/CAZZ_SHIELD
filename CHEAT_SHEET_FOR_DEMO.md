> **Don't worry! You don't need to know complex code or advanced math to give an amazing demo.**

---

## 🧠 THE 3 SIMPLE THINGS TO REMEMBER ABOUT YOUR APP

If the judges ask you *"What did you build?"*, just remember these 3 core ideas:

1. **"It's a Monitoring System"**: It watches 2,500 AI bots that banks use to move money and trade stocks.
2. **"It's an Automatic Security Guard"**: If a bot behaves bad, the system automatically lowers its **Trust Score** and cuts its **Spending Budget**.
3. **"It Has a Big Emergency Kill-Switch"**: If a hack or AI glitch happens, 1 click stops all 2,500 bots instantly.

## 🖥️ SCREEN-BY-SCREEN CHEAT SHEET (What You Are Looking At & Saying)

---

### 1️⃣ LOGIN PAGE (`http://localhost:5173/login`)

- **What it is**: The security entrance to the app.
- **Where to point your mouse**: Point at the 5 role cards (*Enterprise Administrator, Security Officer, Compliance Auditor*).
- **What to click**: Click **"Security Officer"**, then click **"Authenticate & Launch Control Plane"**.
- **What to say**:
  > *"This is the login page. In a bank, different people have different access permissions. I am logging in right now as a **Security Officer**."*

---

### 2️⃣ DASHBOARD (`http://localhost:5173/`)

- **What it is**: The main control room overview.
- **Where to point your mouse**:
  - Point to the 4 top boxes: **1,850 Active**, **480 Restricted**, **140 Quarantined**, **30 Offline**.
  - Point to **Today's Spend**: `$81,200`.
  - Point to **Decision Latency**: `1.2 ms`.
- **What to say**:
  > *"This is our main Dashboard. It monitors **2,500 AI bots** in real time. 
  > 
  > You can see 1,850 bots are healthy and working. 140 bad bots were automatically quarantined. The system checks every single bot decision in just **1.2 milliseconds** so it never slows down the bank."*

---

### 3️⃣ AGENT FLEET (`http://localhost:5173/agents`)

- **What it is**: The list of all 2,500 AI bots in the bank.
- **Where to point your mouse**:
  - Point to the **Search Bar**.
  - Click on any row in the table (for example, *SWIFT Agent #1*).
- **What to click**: Type `"Treasury"` in search, then click a table row to open the **Slide-Out Drawer** on the right side of the screen.
- **What to say**:
  > *"This is our Agent Fleet list. We can filter through all 2,500 bots by department — like Treasury or Wire Transfers. 
  > 
  > When I click on any bot, this drawer slides out showing its trust score, owner team, and connected bank APIs."*

---

### 4️⃣ TRUST ENGINE (`http://localhost:5173/trust`)

- **What it is**: The screen that calculates how safe each bot is.
- **Where to point your mouse**:
  - Point to the big circular gauge in the middle: **84.6% (Fleet Average Trust)**.
  - Point to the mathematical formula box on the left.
- **What to say in plain English**:
  > *"This is the Trust Engine. It calculates a live **Trust Score** for every bot. 
  > 
  > If a bot successfully completes transactions, its trust score goes UP. If a bot makes mistakes or breaks rules, its trust score goes DOWN automatically."*

---

### 5️⃣ ADAPTIVE BUDGETS (`http://localhost:5173/budget`)

- **What it is**: The screen that controls how much money bots are allowed to spend.
- **Where to point your mouse**:
  - Point to **Today's Fleet Spend**: `$81,200`.
  - Point to **Cap**: `$100,000`.
- **What to say in plain English**:
  > *"This is the Budget Engine. High-trust bots get higher spending limits, while low-trust bots get their budgets cut to a 5% safety floor so they can't waste bank money."*

---

### 6️⃣ GRAPH INTELLIGENCE (`http://localhost:5173/graph`)

- **What it is**: A visual map showing how bots, banks, and APIs connect together.
- **Where to point your mouse**: Point to the orange button at top right: **"Highlight Suspicious Cluster"**.
- **What to click**: CLICK **"Highlight Suspicious Cluster"**.
- **What happens on screen**: Red collusion nodes light up on the map!
- **What to say**:
  > *"This is Graph Intelligence. It maps all bots, bank accounts, and APIs as an interactive network. 
  > 
  > When I click **'Highlight Suspicious Cluster'**, the system automatically highlights two suspicious bots trying to send duplicate money transfers to the same bank node."*

---

### 7️⃣ EMERGENCY CONTROLS (`http://localhost:5173/emergency`) ⭐ *THE BEST MOMENT*

- **What it is**: The big red panic button screen.
- **Where to point your mouse**: Point to the giant red button: **"FLEET EMERGENCY STOP (KILL SWITCH)"**.
- **What to click**: CLICK **"FLEET EMERGENCY STOP"**.
- **What happens on screen**: The ENTIRE app flashes and turns into red animated emergency mode with a big warning banner across the top header!
- **What to say**:
  > *"This is the most important page: **Emergency Controls**. 
  > 
  > If a cyber attack or AI glitch happens, a Security Admin clicks this red button **[CLICK IT NOW]**. 
  > 
  > In less than 1 millisecond, all 2,500 bots freeze, spending drops to $0, and all API keys are revoked. Once the threat is clear, clicking 'Resume Operations' brings everything safely back online."*

- **Where to point your mouse**: Point to the prompt pills below the chat box.

- **What to say**:
